# Legal and policy assumptions

The engine supplies reproducible calculations and privacy guards. It does not
decide which legal regime applies, whether jobs are equal or of equal value,
whether a difference is objectively justified, or whether documentation and
consultation duties have been met.

National implementations of EU pay-transparency rules may differ. Integrators
must version jurisdiction profiles and retain the profile, input reference date,
pay basis, threshold and currency with every frozen report.

The exported Swedish payroll-tax constant and the three-year correction-plan
convention are policy helpers, not universal defaults.
